# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, so new joiners receive a pro-rated amount based on their joining date.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Any balance above 5 days expires on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## How to request leave

Submit requests through the HR portal. Requests longer than 3 days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your manager.
