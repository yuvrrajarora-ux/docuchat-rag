# Onboarding Guide

## Before day one

Your laptop is shipped to you before your start date, and your accounts are created in advance. You will receive a welcome email with your first-week schedule.

## First week

Each new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy who answers day-to-day questions. Day one covers HR formalities and a team introduction. Days two to five cover tools, product overview and shadowing sessions.

## 30-60-90 day plan

Your manager will agree a 30-60-90 day plan with you in the first week. The first 30 days focus on learning, the next 30 on contributing to small tasks, and the final 30 on owning a piece of work.

## Probation and learning

The probation period is 6 months, with a formal review at the end of month 3 and month 6. Every employee has a learning budget of Rs 40,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
