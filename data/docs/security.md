# Information Security Guidelines

## Accounts and passwords

All employees must use the company password manager and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every work account. Never share passwords, even with colleagues or IT staff.

## Phishing and incidents

Report any suspicious email to security@acme.example within 1 hour. Do not click links or open attachments in messages you are unsure about. If you clicked something suspicious, report it immediately; you will not be penalised for reporting quickly.

## Devices

Company laptops are encrypted by default and must not be modified. Personal USB drives are not allowed on company devices. If a laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk immediately so it can be wiped remotely.

When using public Wi-Fi, always connect through the company VPN.

## Training

Security awareness training is mandatory and is held every quarter. Completion is tracked, and repeated non-completion is escalated to your manager.
