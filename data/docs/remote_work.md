# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule

Acme follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office three days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Mondays and Fridays are flexible remote days.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time. Everyone should be reachable during these hours, even when working remotely.

## Home office support

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of Rs 25,000 for a desk, chair or monitor. The company also reimburses internet costs up to Rs 1,000 per month on submission of the bill.

## Working from another city

Employees may work from a different city or country for up to 20 days per year. This requires written approval from your manager at least 3 weeks in advance. Working from another country may have tax implications and must also be cleared with HR.
